💰 Money-making tips

Use Lester’s assassination missions strategicallyStory Mode · Investing⌄
  1. Progress through the story until the assassination missions become available.
  2. Complete the first required assassination mission when the story asks you to.
  3. Consider saving optional assassination missions until after the main story, when your characters may have more money to invest.
  4. Save manually before investing, and check the in-game market before and after each mission.

💡 Stock prices vary; there is no guaranteed return.

Earn cash through heistsStory Mode · Missions⌄
  1. Advance the story to unlock heist planning missions.
  2. Review the available crew members and their costs before choosing.
  3. Complete optional preparation tasks when offered.
  4. Finish the heist and review your payout; different choices can affect results.

💡 Heist rewards depend on story progress and choices.

Keep your money after expensive purchasesStory Mode · Budgeting⌄
  1. Make a manual save before buying vehicles or property.
  2. Compare the purchase with the cash needed for your next mission.
  3. Avoid buying replacements for vehicles you can recover or store.
  4. Use a separate save slot before experimenting with costly purchases.

💡 This helps you avoid losing progress while experimenting.

💡 Aircraft availability and restricted areas depend on game progress.

⭐ Police escape tricks

Break the line of sightStory Mode · Wanted level⌄
  1. Watch the minimap for police positions and their search area.
  2. Turn down side streets or move behind buildings to get out of sight.
  3. Avoid driving directly toward police icons while the search is active.
  4. Stay out of view until the wanted stars stop flashing and disappear.

💡 The technique works best when you avoid being spotted again.

Use tunnels and covered routesStory Mode · Map knowledge⌄
  1. Open the map and identify an underpass, tunnel, or covered route nearby.
  2. Head there during a wanted-level chase in the game.
  3. Stay away from police patrols and avoid blocking your own exit.
  4. Wait for the search to end before returning to busy streets.

💡 No hiding place is guaranteed to work every time.

Switch vehicles after losing sightStory Mode · Free roam⌄
  1. First get away from the immediate search area.
  2. When it is safe in-game, leave the vehicle you were using.
  3. Switch to another available vehicle and take a different route.
  4. Continue avoiding patrols until your wanted level clears.

💡 Changing vehicles alone will not instantly erase wanted stars.

🎮 Beginner & advanced tricks

Use each character’s special abilityStory Mode · Controls⌄
  1. Switch to Michael, Franklin, or Trevor in Story Mode.
  2. Check the controls menu for the special ability input on your platform.
  3. Practice using Franklin’s driving focus, Michael’s combat focus, or Trevor’s ability during gameplay.
  4. Allow the ability meter to recharge before using it again.

💡 Each protagonist has a different ability.

Improve your driving and stunt landingsStory Mode · Driving⌄
  1. Choose a familiar vehicle and find a wide, open road or stunt area.
  2. Approach corners smoothly and avoid steering too sharply at high speed.
  3. For jumps, line up straight before reaching the ramp.
  4. Adjust your approach and speed if you miss the landing.

💡 Control and timing often matter more than top speed.

Make exploration easier with waypointsStory Mode · Navigation⌄
  1. Pause the game and open the map.
  2. Set a waypoint to the destination you want to visit.
  3. Follow the highlighted route, checking the map for shorter alternatives.
  4. Save interesting locations as a personal list outside the game for later visits.

💡 Waypoints help you explore without memorizing every road.
